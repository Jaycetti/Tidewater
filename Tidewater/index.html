Loose-leaf tea, shipped within days of blending

Tidewater Tea Co. is a proposed online shop for small-batch teas, seasonal blends, and simple brewing gear. We buy direct from growers and pack orders to order, so every tin arrives fresh.

Browse the tea collection

What the platform offers

Customers will be able to browse teas by type, read tasting notes and brewing instructions, and order online. Planned products include:

  • Single-origin black, green, oolong, and herbal teas
  • Seasonal blends and tea sampler sets
  • Teapots, infusers, and gift boxes
  • Monthly tea subscriptions

Who it is for

Home tea drinkers who want better quality than grocery-store bags, gift buyers looking for something thoughtful, and small cafés that need dependable wholesale supply.
